# Week 1 Learning Checklist

Complete these without relying on the implementation as your only explanation.

1. Draw the target's LVLH frame and explain all six state variables and signs.
2. Derive the mean motion expression and compute the period for a 500 km circular orbit.
3. Explain the physical meaning of the `3 n^2 x`, `2 n y_dot`, `-2 n x_dot`, and `-n^2 z` terms.
4. Explain why the chaser can drift away even with *zero control thrust*.
5. Predict qualitatively how changing `x0`, `vy0`, and `z0` changes the trajectory, then run experiments.
6. Compare numerical integration and analytic propagation: what does agreement verify, and what doesn't it verify?
7. Make a commit documenting one finding in `docs/engineering_log.md`.

**Interview check:** Explain the simulation in 45 seconds without looking at the code.
